# The Everlasting: condensed edition, what was cut

The condensed edition is about 19,100 words, one third of the full 57,610. Dialogue that stayed is word for word from the manuscript, with dialect spellings intact; narration was trimmed and lightly tightened. The full edition is untouched and can be switched back on in the Studio (Story details, "Edition readers see").

This list covers the cuts most likely to matter. Anything here can be restored scene by scene.

## Chapter One (episodes 1 to 3)
- Festival business detail: Thump TV sponsorship, resort layout, the $24 million figure. Niara stays as the Esta-owey event planner; William Rutledge III is reduced to a named developer.
- Policing numbers and Aiyana's thought of firing Sharp. Adair leading the SLED task force stays.
- The boardroom flashback loses Aunt Fey and the old chief questioning Caleb. Sakima's green eyes and biting grip stay.
- Lucas's Gullah exchange in the car; he is now introduced by name only, as one of the twins.

## Chapter Two (episodes 4 to 6)
- The description of Levi's house, and most of the teenage lighthouse memory with Caleb.
- Vivi's "one long transition" outburst and the rest of Lucas's speech about reading the unknown words.
- Eva's sleep-talk ("Don't do it! Help him!") and Vivi's floating shot glass.
- Niara's set-up schedule; her and Adair's physical descriptions.

## Chapter Three (episodes 7 to 10)
- Caleb and Aiyana's exchange about her grandmother's prognosis.
- Rosalind and Lucas meeting at the buffet; "art and marine biology".
- The "meeting Manci at six" line and Eva's payoff joke.
- Aiyana's reservations about year-round traffic on the island.

## Chapters Four and Five (episodes 11 to 16)
- The "day I left" exchange, and with it any mention in these episodes that Caleb is studying to be a doctor.
- The love scene is reduced to a few beats.
- The cove conversation (four years on the island, New York and the stars).
- Uncle Max officiating the transfer of power; Harold introducing Aiyana as the next Gullah queen.
- Bonfire policing detail just before the body is found.

## Chapters Six and Seven (episodes 17 to 23)
- The drug-source exchange (mainland pushers at the Esta-owey casino, Caleb's odd look), and the salt-water DNA line.
- Mrs. Rutledge flying in at four; Adair's apology for assuming Aiyana would want a cover-up.
- Sakima's angry grip on Aiyana as a child (second mention).
- Aunt Helen's funeral arrangements and the Esta-owey clothes request.
- Most of the press statement.

## Chapters Eight and Nine (episodes 24 to 30)
- Caleb showing the fading handprints (they now first appear at the reveal in episode 25).
- The siblings' alibis for Mr. Rutledge; Eva's second vision.
- Aunt Helen's speech about the handmaid's lies and Charii's protection spell.
- The elders blaming the handmaiden; the Esta-owey caging the possessed until the blood moon passes.
- Captain Gamelle's backstory is now one line of narration.
- "Up to three hours" possession length; Aiyana's defence of Caleb.

## Chapters Ten to Thirteen (episodes 31 to 40)
- Charii's self-introduction ("They call me Charii...").
- Adair's absence from the ceremony and the security posting.
- Sakima's "Magic is magic" exchange.
- Helen's "I thought it was temporary" admission.
- Sakima's history: eminent domain, the four shamans' descendants, the handmaiden's death.
- The siblings' signs and the "Charii could not possess your mother" exchange.
- The ferry callback (his touch sedating her).
- Lucas's "She wanted to be free"; "I was always meant to end up here alone".

## Chapters Fourteen to Seventeen (episodes 41 to 47)
- The demon crushing the cameras and altering what witnesses saw.
- Diary details: Malinda's siblings leaving, the demon pacing at the Esta-owey line.
- Parts of Aiyana's confession to Adair; his surveillance plan.
- Sakima's closing speech that Lucas's full text could show how to bind the spirit again.
- "The plan originated with our elders."
- Winterton's question about why Sakima would meet at the family house if he lives at the casino.

## Chapters Eighteen to Twenty-One (episodes 48 to 52)
- Dr. Howard's toxin explanation. His "release you tomorrow morning" line is kept, right after "keep you here until after that"; the two contradict each other in the manuscript and are left for you to resolve.
- "Your mother blocked the spirit, somehow."
- Escape logistics (the bribe, the medic's lines).
- Caleb's report of Adair questioning him in New York, the internet video, and Mrs. Rutledge's investigator.
- Aiyana's dying thoughts and the reflection on Charii's portrait.
- The Caleb ending is unchanged. The Sakima ending keeps the whole hospital visit as written.

## Other things to know
- A few scenes keep one bridging sentence of narration that is mine, not the manuscript's (for example "Mrs. Rutledge was calm."). These are short and drawn from the original wording.
- Where a speech lost its mid-line "she said", the two halves are joined into one quote.
- Manuscript quirks left as written: "onto to the balcony", "reign down", and Sakima saying both "your father had five bloodlines" and "your mother had the five bloodlines inside of her".
- The missing closing quote after Aunt Helen's "She fell and broke her neck." is supplied in the condensed edition.
